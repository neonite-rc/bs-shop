Our Story

Gifting, with a little more heart

Brothers & Sisters Gift Shop, based in Bhubaneswar.

We started with a simple idea: finding the right gift should feel as good as giving it. So we gathered the little things we love — handcrafted showpieces, soft plush, charming home decor and keepsakes that last — and put them all under one roof.

Every item in our collection is chosen for a reason. Whether it's a birthday, a wedding, a festival or a just-because Tuesday, we'll help you find something that feels personal — and wrap it like it means it.

  • Handpicked with careNothing mass-picked — each piece earns its place.
  • Beautifully wrappedFree gift wrap and a handwritten note on every order.
  • Personalised touchesNames, messages and little details that make it theirs.
